Flip Web: Share files with any device through a browser.

Flip Web lets your Android phone send and receive files, folders, and text with any other phone, tablet, or computer. The other person does not need to install an app. They only need a regular web browser like Safari, Chrome, or Edge.

What Flip Web Does in Plain Terms

Designed for everyday sharing when the other device does not have Flip installed.

Nothing to Install on the Other Side

Only your Android phone needs the Flip app. The other person can be on an iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows PC, or another phone and simply open their browser.

Zero Mobile Data or Internet Needed

Your files do not go to the internet or a cloud server. They travel directly between the two devices in the same room over Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot.

Works Both Ways (Send and Receive)

You can send photos, videos, apps, and documents from your Android phone to the browser, or upload files and folders from the browser straight to your phone.

Instant Text and Link Sharing

Need to send a long link, password, or note to a computer or iPhone? Type or paste it in the Text tab and copy it with one tap on the other screen.

How to Use Flip Web (Step by Step)

Follow these five simple steps to connect and transfer files in seconds.

Step 1

Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi (or turn on your Hotspot)

Make sure your Android phone and the other device are connected to the same home or office Wi-Fi.

No Wi-Fi router nearby? Turn on Mobile Hotspot in your Android phone settings and connect the other device to your phone's Wi-Fi network. You can keep mobile data turned off because no internet is used.

Step 2

Tap the Web icon in Flip on your Android phone

Open the Flip app on your Android phone and tap the Web (globe) icon on the home screen. A panel will open showing a QR code and a short link (for example, http://192.168.43.1:8080).

Step 3

Scan the QR code or open the short link on the other device

On another phone or tablet (such as an iPhone or iPad): Open the regular camera app, point it at the QR code on your Android screen, and tap the web link that appears.

On a laptop or desktop computer: Open any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox), type the short link shown on your Android phone into the top address bar, and press Enter.

Step 4

Send files, folders, or text in either direction

Once the page opens in the browser, both devices are connected:

  • Phone to Browser: Select any files, photos, videos, documents, or apps on your Android phone. They show up in the browser immediately so the other person can tap Download.
  • Browser to Phone: Tap Send Files (or drag and drop files and folders) in the browser. Your Android phone will ask you to approve incoming files before saving them to your storage.
  • Share Text: Open the Text tab on either screen to type or paste notes and web links back and forth.
Step 5

Tap Disconnect when you are done

When your transfer finishes, tap Disconnect on either your Android phone or the browser page. Flip immediately closes the connection on both devices.

Common Questions

Quick answers before you start transferring.

Will this use up my mobile data plan?

No. Even if you use your phone's hotspot to connect the two devices, the files travel directly from one device to the other through the local Wi-Fi signal without touching your mobile data.

Why is Web Mode started from Android only?

The Android Flip app runs a built-in local web server right on your phone. That allows any other device with a browser (including iPhones and Macs) to connect to your Android phone without installing anything.

Can someone send files to my phone without my permission?

No. Whenever someone uploads files from their browser to your phone, your Android screen shows an approval prompt first so you can accept or decline the files.

What happens if my Wi-Fi or hotspot turns off?

Flip monitors your network address in real time. If the hotspot is turned off or the Wi-Fi changes, the browser immediately shows "Connection lost" so neither device gets stuck waiting.
